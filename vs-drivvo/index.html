Switching

Bring your history with you

  1. Export your data from Drivvo (Settings → Export/Backup).
  2. Open AutoMate → Import → select the exported file.
  3. Your fuel logs, service history, and vehicles carry over — nothing to re-enter by hand.
Who's behind it

One developer, not a growth team

AutoMate is built and maintained by a single developer. There's no roadmap built around upsells, no A/B-tested paywall timing, no growth team deciding when to start showing ads. The incentive is simply to make the app good enough that you keep using it.
