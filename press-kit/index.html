Descriptions

Short: AutoMate is an ad-free, offline car expense and fuel tracker — no account required, all data stored on your device.

Long: AutoMate helps drivers track fuel efficiency, maintenance, and expenses across one or several vehicles without ads, accounts, or a cloud subscription. Every fill-up, service record, and expense is stored locally on the device — nothing is uploaded unless the user explicitly exports it. AutoMate supports multi-vehicle and small-fleet use, mileage- or date-based maintenance reminders, CSV/PDF export, and easy import from Drivvo and Fuelio. The app is available in 7 languages and is built and maintained by a solo developer.
