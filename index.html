What it does

Four instruments, one dashboard.

Fuel efficiency tracking

Log every fill-up and watch your real MPG or L/100km trend over time — by tank, by month, by vehicle.

All-in-one expense tracking

Fuel, service, and general expenses in one ledger. Multi-vehicle and small-fleet support built in from day one.

Non-intrusive reminders

Maintenance reminders by mileage or by date — tire rotations, oil changes, registration. Set once, forget until it matters.

CSV / PDF export, easy import

Export your full history anytime. Switching from Fuelio or Drivvo? Import your existing records in a few taps.

Why it's built this way

Most free trackers monetize you. This one doesn't.

Ad-supported and freemium car-tracking apps pay for themselves with your attention and your data — banner ads between entries, subscription walls around basic export, or your usage patterns sold to advertisers. AutoMate has none of that: no ad SDKs, no account to create, no server your data has to round-trip through. Your fuel logs and service history live in a local database on your phone. If you export a CSV, that's the only copy that ever leaves the device — and only because you asked for it.
